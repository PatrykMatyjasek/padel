# What's coming to Padel Manager

Here's a look at what we're working on next. No hard dates yet — we'd rather ship it right than ship it fast.

---

## Tournament series & season standings

Run a weekly club night or a monthly circuit? You'll be able to group tournaments into a **series** and track a live season leaderboard across all of them. Choose whether every event counts, only your best results, or drop your worst score. The series will have its own shareable link so the whole club can follow the standings.

---

## Player profiles & shareable stats

Your personal stats page — points, wins, win rate, podium finishes, best partners, toughest rivals — will get a **public shareable link**. Send it to anyone; no login required to view it. Show off your record after a big win.

---

## Skill rating (Elo)

Raw points favour players who simply play more. We're adding an **Elo-based skill rating** that adjusts after every tournament based on how strong your opponents were. Beat a top-ranked team? Big rating jump. Lose to a stronger pair? Only a small drop. Your rating will appear on your profile and on series leaderboards alongside the traditional points table.

---

## Live score updates

Watching a tournament via the shared link currently requires a manual refresh. Scores will soon **update automatically** — useful for spectators following from courtside or players checking how other matches are going.

---

## Classic format improvements

A few quality-of-life upgrades for Classic (fixed teams) tournaments:

- **Winners advance automatically** after each knockout round — no manual bracket entry.
- **Court numbers shown** on every match so players know exactly where to go.
- When all matches in a Mexicano round are done, the app will **prompt you to generate the next round** instead of requiring you to find the button yourself.

---

## Score entry that survives bad WiFi

If your connection drops while entering scores, the app will hold your input and retry automatically when you're back online — no lost data, no "please refresh" errors at the worst possible moment.

---

## Player accounts (claim your profile)

Right now players exist only inside an organizer's account. We're exploring letting players **create their own accounts** and claim their match history across all tournaments they've played in — regardless of who organized them.

---

We ship improvements continuously, so some of these may arrive sooner than others. If there's something specific you'd like to see — or a bug that's driving you mad — hit the **feedback button** in the app.
